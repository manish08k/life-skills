## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Unwanted touching, inappropriate comments, sexual jokes, gestures, messages, unwanted advances, or asking for sexual favours. Any unwelcome behaviour that makes someone uncomfortable can be sexual harassment.

## 2. What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?

I would speak up if it is safe, note down what happened, and report it to HR or the Internal Committee. If I witness it, I would support the affected person and encourage them to report it. I would never ignore such behaviour.
