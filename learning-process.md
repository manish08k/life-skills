# Questions and Answers

### Question 1
If we want to learn fast, we should understand things first. One way to do this is by explaining them to someone else, which helps us understand better.

### Question 2
The interesting thing in the story was that she gave it a try. Maybe it could be the most suitable thing for her. We don't know unless we try. By giving it a try, she understood that she was also good at Maths and Science. Just because something feels hard doesn't mean it is bad.

### Question 3
Active mode is used when thinking about a particular thing, such as solving a problem or doing a task. Passive mode is when we think freely, which helps us discover new things and be creative.

### Question 4
First, learn the basics and understand what is needed before we start. This helps us understand what to do next. We should also divide things into smaller parts and keep practising for a minimum of 20 hours.

### Question 5
Focus on one thing and keep distractions aside. Understand the concepts before learning further and set a deadline. Try to learn and focus on one thing rather than trying to learn everything at once.
