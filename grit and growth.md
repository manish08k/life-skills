# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

Grit means having passion and perseverance towards our goals.
Even when things become difficult or we fail, we should keep trying and not give up until we achieve what we want.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills and knowledge through hard work, learning and practice.
Instead of being afraid of failure, we should treat it as an opportunity to learn.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions and efforts are responsible for our results.
The key point is that instead of blaming others or situations, we should take responsibility and focus on what we can control.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

1. Believe that abilities can be improved.
2. Accept challenges.
3. Learn from mistakes and failures.
4. Take feedback positively.
5. Keep practising and improving.
6. Focus on the learning process.
7. Be consistent and never give up.
8. Celebrate small improvements.
9. Learn from others.
10. Step out of your comfort zone.

## 5. My Ideas to Take Action and Build a Growth Mindset

I will try to learn something new every day.
I will improve my technical skills through regular practice.
Whenever I face a difficult task or make mistakes, I will try to understand what went wrong instead of giving up.
I will take feedback from others and work on my weak areas.
I will avoid comparing my progress with others.
My main focus will be on improving myself step by step and staying consistent with my goals.
